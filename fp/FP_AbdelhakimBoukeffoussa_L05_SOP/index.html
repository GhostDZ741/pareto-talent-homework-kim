SOP · Launching a lead magnet funnel for Pareto Talent

FP | Abdelhakim Boukeffoussa | SOP · Version 1.0 · October 2026 · Owner: Abdelhakim Boukeffoussa

Purpose

Launch a lead magnet funnel that turns founder traffic into qualified Matching Calls, the same way every time, in 4 working days.

Scope

Covers everything from research to a tested funnel: lead magnet, landing page, qualifying form, booking page, thank-you page, CRM pipeline, 4 workflows and 5 ads. Does NOT cover: ad spend and media buying, sales call scripts, or Right Hand onboarding after the sale.

Definitions

  • Qualified: monthly revenue $50K+ AND 10+ hours a week on admin, inbox and follow-ups.
  • Lights: green = the Right Hand decides and does · yellow = prepares, founder approves · red = founder keeps.
  • Black Box brief: goal, context, constraints, definition of done, decision latitude.
  • Naming rule: everything inside tools is called "FP | Firstname Lastname | Item Name". Nothing is left as Copy, Untitled or Workflow 3.

Tools

Claude (research + copy + build), GoHighLevel (form, calendar, pages, pipeline, workflows), GitHub Pages (lead magnet hosting), ChatGPT image (ad imagery), ClickUp (project board), Loom (presentation).

Procedure

Day 1 · Research and offer (about 4 hours)

  1. Read the brief and the official guidelines. Copy the exact link labels into the project doc before building anything.
  2. Research 5+ competitors that fight for the same customer. For each: offer, price, live ads (Meta Ad Library, Google Ads Transparency), and lead magnets.
  3. Mystery-shop 3 competitor lead magnets with a test email alias. Never use a phone number. Record the fields, the qualification and what happens after opt-in.
  4. Build a voice-of-customer bank: real founder quotes with source URLs (reviews, podcasts, forums). Tag each one: pain, objection, trigger, outcome.
  5. Write the ICP in one paragraph and 3–5 personas by cause (the trigger), in client words.
  6. Choose the lead magnet format by finding the gap: what nobody (competitors or Pareto) offers yet.
  7. Name it with the MAGIC formula and check it against the Value Equation. Write the qualification rule and the next-step offer. Done when: the offer, qualification rule and personas are written in one doc and approved.

Day 2 · Build what the founder sees (about 5 hours)

  1. Pull the brand from the live site, not memory: colors, fonts, radius (browser dev tools → computed styles).
  2. Build the lead magnet. It must be useful without ever talking to Pareto. Publish it on a public URL and test it in an incognito window.
  3. Build the landing page in this order: hero, what's inside, who it's for, proof with real Pareto numbers, form.
  4. Build the qualifying form in GoHighLevel: name, email, revenue band, admin hours, biggest bottleneck. Add Conditional Logic → "Redirect to URL":
    • Rule 1: revenue under $50K → thank-you page.
    • Rule 2: admin hours under 10 → thank-you page.
    • Default on-submit redirect → booking page. (Rules run top-down; the first match wins.)
  5. Build the calendar ("Matching Call", 20 minutes, 10-minute buffers) and embed it on the booking page.
  6. Build the thank-you page: confirmation, the link to the resource, next steps, support, and objection handling. Done when: you submit the form twice, as a qualified and an unqualified founder, and land on the right page each time.

Day 3 · CRM, automation and creative (about 5 hours)

  1. Pipeline stages: Opted In → Qualified → Call Booked → Client, plus Unqualified.
  2. Opt-in workflow. Trigger: form submitted, filtered to this form only (the account is shared).
    • Tag the contact (qualified / unqualified).
    • Create the opportunity in the right stage.
    • Send the delivery email.
    • Notify yourself only, never "all users".
    • Re-entry OFF.
  3. Qualified follow-up workflow: 3 emails (day 1, 3, 6). Stop as soon as a call is booked.
  4. Unqualified nurture workflow: 3 emails (day 1, 3, 6).
  5. Booking workflow. Trigger: appointment booked on this calendar. Send a confirmation instantly and a reminder 24 hours before.
  6. Test the full chain with 2 fresh email aliases and no phone numbers (GHL merges duplicates). Screenshot the execution logs, the pipeline and the inbox.
    • For the test only, shorten waits to 2 minutes. Put them back afterwards.
  7. Write 5 ads, each a different persona × angle: hook, offer, qualifier, proof, mechanism, CTA. Generate images with the palette screenshot attached. Save every prompt. Done when: both test leads show every step green in the logs, and 5 ads plus prompts sit in the named folders.

Day 4 · Document and ship (about 4 hours)

  1. Business map from ad to call. Link every step to its real asset. Mark anything missing as a gap; never invent a link.
  2. Update the ClickUp board: every task has an owner, deadline, status and priority.
  3. Record a 2-minute Loom, face on. Start with the result, then walk through the assets.
  4. Write the submission PDF in the exact template order. Full URLs only, no shorteners.
  5. Open EVERY link in an incognito window. Fix anything that asks for a login.
  6. Decide the state of the workflows (on or off) and write that decision and the reason in the PDF. Submit at least 6 hours before the deadline.

Quality checks (before calling anything done)

  • Every number on every page comes from paretotalent.com or a named source. No invented stats.
  • Every link opens in incognito without a login.
  • Every item in every tool follows the naming rule.
  • Both form paths are tested, and the email arrives.

Lessons from the first run (October 2026)

  • GoHighLevel merges contacts with the same email or phone, so tests show "Skipped". Use a fresh alias per test.
  • A 1-day wait makes testing slow. Shorten it for the test, then restore it.
  • The guidelines changed the email counts from the slides (qualified 2 / unqualified 3 vs 3 / 1). Build to the stricter of both.
