Questions founders ask.

Who is this for?

Founders doing $100K+ a month who have become the bottleneck in their own company. It isn't built for solopreneurs or for hiring freelancers.

Where do Right Hands come from?

Latin America. Each placement is chosen from more than 1,000 applicants and completes 40+ hours of AI training before placement.

You answer 2 quick questions (under a minute) and get an email with the next step. On a real Right Hand match, that means a short matching call, then 3 pre-vetted profiles within 24 hours. No contract until you find your person.
